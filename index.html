      What ChairBack will never do

      Invent a price

      It only quotes what's on your rate card. If a price depends on hair length, it asks.

      Promise a discount

      Discount and refund requests are flagged for you. It never says yes on your behalf.

      Confirm a slot

      It can't see your diary, so it never says "booked". It tells you to check.

      Send anything

      Every reply is a draft. You decide what goes to your customer.

      Why not quick replies or ChatGPT?

      Quick replies

      Good for "timings kya hain". You still have to find the right template, and they can't read a message that asks three things at once.

      A general chatbot

      Can write a good reply, but you have to give it your prices and rules every time.

      ChairBack

      Reads the message the way your customer wrote it, checks it against the rules you saved, and shows what is holding the booking back.
